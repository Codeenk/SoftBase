What we collect, and why.

What we collect

Through the client inquiry form: your name, email, and any optional details you choose to share (company, website, phone or WhatsApp number, project description, current workflow, integration requirements, timeline, and the budget you have in mind). Through the internship application form: your name, email, and optional contact details, education information, availability, links you choose to share, and your answers to the application questions.

Why we collect it

The only purposes are to respond to your inquiry, to evaluate internship applications, and to communicate with you about them. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are recorded in private spreadsheets accessible only to SoftBase. Submissions are transmitted through a secure server endpoint; credentials for that endpoint are never exposed in the browser.

Your choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete the information you submitted at any time, by writing to the relevant address below. We act on reasonable requests promptly.

Contact

For client inquiries: build@softbase.services. For internship applications: careers@softbase.services.
